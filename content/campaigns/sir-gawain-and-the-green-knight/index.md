# Sir Gawain and the Green Knight — campaign bundle

A Bardcast campaign seed, projected as an [OKF](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/knowledge-catalog/blob/main/okf/SPEC.md)
bundle. Source: the anonymous 14th-century Middle English poem (public domain).
All prose here is original summary; no copyrighted translation is reproduced.

This is **world + quest canon** — Bardcast-staff-authored, licensing-reviewed,
read-only to DM clients (see `docs/story-engine.md §1`). Character and chapter
canon are not here; they accrue from play.
