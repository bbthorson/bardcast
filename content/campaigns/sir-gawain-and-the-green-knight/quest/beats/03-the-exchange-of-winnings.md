## Setting
[Castle Hautdesert](../../world/places/castle-hautdesert.md), three midwinter days
before the appointment.

## What happens
[Lord Bertilak](../../world/npcs/lord-bertilak.md) proposes the exchange of winnings
and rides out to hunt each day; meanwhile
[Lady Bertilak](../../world/npcs/lady-bertilak.md) visits the chamber to tempt the
guest. Each evening, winnings are exchanged. On the third day the guest accepts the
[green girdle](../../world/items/the-green-girdle.md) and **conceals it**.

## The central choice
*Courtesy vs. self-preservation.* Refuse the lady without insult; honor the exchange
honestly — or keep the life-saving girdle secret. This is the heart of the whole
campaign, and the richest beat for **behavior** signal (how the character resolves a
values conflict).

## Resolution notes
Social **checks** (persuasion/resolve) keyed to sheet traits, run as three parallel
threads (the intercut hunts and temptations). The concealment is a **player choice**
that becomes a decision-node checkpoint — it directly determines the nick at the
chapel. Failure here is moral/social, not lethal.

→ leads to [Beat 4 — The Green Chapel](./04-the-green-chapel.md).
