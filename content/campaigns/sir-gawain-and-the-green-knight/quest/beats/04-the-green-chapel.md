## Setting
The [Green Chapel](../../world/places/the-green-chapel.md) — the eerie mound by the
loud stream, on the appointed day.

## What happens
The knight kneels for the return blow. The
[Green Knight](../../world/npcs/the-green-knight.md) feints twice and on the third
stroke leaves only a small nick — then reveals himself as
[Lord Bertilak](../../world/npcs/lord-bertilak.md). The three axe-strokes answer the
three days of temptation; the nick is the price of the concealed
[girdle](../../world/items/the-green-girdle.md). He names
[Morgan le Fay](../../world/npcs/morgan-le-fay.md) as the architect and judges the
knight kindly — flawed, but the truest of men.

## The central choice
*Does the character flinch — and how do they meet the truth of their small failing?*
Prompts about courage at the block, and about shame, relief, or grace afterward.

## Resolution notes
The outcome is **set by Beat 3's choices**, not a fresh roll — concealing the girdle
yields the nick; perfect honesty yields none. The danger is theatrical: this is the
canonical example of a mortal-seeming moment that is, by design, a **setback (a
scar, a lesson), not a death**. If generation ever drives toward a real death here,
it backtracks to the Beat 3 decision node (`docs/story-engine.md §4`).

End of the seed arc. New campaigns continue from the knight's changed self.
