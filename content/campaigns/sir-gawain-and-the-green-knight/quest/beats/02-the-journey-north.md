## Setting
The [Wilderness of Wirral](../../world/places/the-wilderness-of-wirral.md) in deep
winter, as the appointed time nears.

## What happens
The knight travels north through cold, hunger, and danger — skirmishes, sheer
country, freezing nights. At the edge of despair he prays for shelter and
[Castle Hautdesert](../../world/places/castle-hautdesert.md) appears in the snow.

## The central choice
*How does the character endure — and who do they become on a hard road alone?*
Prompts about fear, faith, doubt, and resolve. Strong beat for **voice** capture
(soliloquy, prayer) and **behavior** signal (how they handle privation).

## Resolution notes
Survival/endurance **checks** keyed to sheet traits. Failures are **setbacks** — a
lost supply, a wound, a lost day — never death. The beat ends safely at the castle
gate regardless; the rolls color *how battered* the character arrives.
