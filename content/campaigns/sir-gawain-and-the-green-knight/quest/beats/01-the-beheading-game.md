## Setting
The New Year's feast at [Camelot](../../world/places/camelot.md). Warmth and
boasting, the court at ease.

## What happens
The [Green Knight](../../world/npcs/the-green-knight.md) rides in and proposes the
bargain (a blow now for a blow in a year and a day). A knight of the party accepts,
takes up the [axe](../../world/items/the-axe.md), and beheads him — and he rises,
collects his head, and names the appointment.

## The central choice
*Who steps forward, and how?* This is the chapter that establishes each
player-knight. Good DM prompts: "the hall has fallen silent and the giant offers
his axe — what do you do?"; "the head speaks your name — what's going through your
mind?" Replies here are **anchors and backstory**; the chapter expands well past them.

## Resolution notes
Largely roleplay; little to fail. If the strike is rolled, a fumble is theatrical
(an ugly blow), not lethal — the Green Knight cannot truly die here.
