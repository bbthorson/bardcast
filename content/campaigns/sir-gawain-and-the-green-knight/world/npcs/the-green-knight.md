An enormous knight, his skin and clothing and horse all green, who rides armed only
with an [axe](../items/the-axe.md) into [Camelot](../places/camelot.md) and proposes
his game: a blow now for a blow in a year and a day at the
[Green Chapel](../places/the-green-chapel.md). Beheaded by Gawain, he calmly lifts
his own severed head, which repeats the appointment, and rides away.

**Twist (staff canon, withhold from players):** he is
[Lord Bertilak](./lord-bertilak.md), transformed by
[Morgan le Fay](./morgan-le-fay.md). He is less a villain than an examiner — his
whole purpose is to test the honor of the [Round Table](../factions/the-round-table.md).
He threatens but, by design, does **not** intend a true death — a natural fit for
Bardcast's death-as-rare-constraint rule.
