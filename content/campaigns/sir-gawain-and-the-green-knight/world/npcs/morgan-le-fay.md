The enchantress who engineered the entire ordeal. By the poem's late revelation,
she transformed [Lord Bertilak](./lord-bertilak.md) into the
[Green Knight](./the-green-knight.md) and set the test in motion — partly to prove
the [Round Table's](../factions/the-round-table.md) renown hollow, partly to
frighten Arthur's queen.

**Role in play.** The unseen hand, not an encounter. She explains *why* the world
behaved as it did, and exists in staff canon to keep the campaign's logic coherent
behind the curtain. Reveal only at or after [the climax](../places/the-green-chapel.md).
