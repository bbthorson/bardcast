The robust, big-bearded lord of [Castle Hautdesert](../places/castle-hautdesert.md),
overflowing with hospitality and fond of the hunt. He proposes the **exchange of
winnings**: each day he gives Gawain his catch from the field, and Gawain gives him
whatever he has gained indoors.

For three days he hunts (deer, then a boar, then a fox) while
[his lady](./lady-bertilak.md) tests Gawain at home. **Twist (staff canon):** he and
the [Green Knight](./the-green-knight.md) are one and the same — the hunts and the
bedroom temptations are the *real* test, the axe at the
[Green Chapel](../places/the-green-chapel.md) merely its verdict. He bears Gawain no
true malice and ultimately judges him kindly.
