The gracious and clever lady of [Castle Hautdesert](../places/castle-hautdesert.md).
While her lord hunts, she visits Gawain's chamber each morning, pressing courtship
on him. The contest is one of **courtesy under pressure**: he must refuse her
without insulting her — a social tightrope, not a physical danger.

On the third day she gives him the [green girdle](../items/the-green-girdle.md),
claiming it will protect his life. Gawain accepts it and — breaking the exchange
game — conceals it from [her lord](./lord-bertilak.md). She acts at the direction of
[Morgan le Fay](./morgan-le-fay.md); the temptation is engineered.
