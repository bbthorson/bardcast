High King of Britain, holding court at [Camelot](../places/camelot.md). In this
tale he is youthful and high-spirited, with a custom of not feasting until he has
heard of some marvel. When the [Green Knight](../npcs/the-green-knight.md) issues
his challenge and the hall sits stunned, Arthur himself moves to take the axe —
until Gawain asks to take the blow in his place.

**Role in play.** A patron and moral anchor, not an adventurer in the field. Useful
for framing the stakes ("the honor of my whole court rides on this") and for
chapter-one prompts at the feast.
