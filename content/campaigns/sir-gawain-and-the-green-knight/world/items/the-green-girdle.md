A green silk girdle (sash) offered to Gawain by [Lady Bertilak](../npcs/lady-bertilak.md)
on the third morning, with the claim that whoever wears it cannot be slain. Out of
fear for his life at the coming reckoning, Gawain accepts it **and conceals it** from
[the lord](../npcs/lord-bertilak.md), breaking their exchange-of-winnings game.

This is the campaign's moral fulcrum: not a crime, but a small lapse born of the
will to live. At the [Green Chapel](../places/the-green-chapel.md) it accounts for
the single nick the Green Knight leaves on Gawain's neck. Gawain afterward wears it
as a badge of his fault; Camelot adopts it in solidarity. A perfect model for
Bardcast's "good people fall a little short" beat — a **setback that marks a
character**, not a death.
