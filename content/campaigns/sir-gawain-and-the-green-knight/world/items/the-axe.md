An enormous, gleaming axe carried by the [Green Knight](../npcs/the-green-knight.md)
into [Camelot](../places/camelot.md). It is the literal instrument of the beheading
game: Gawain strikes the first blow with it, and a year later kneels beneath it at
the [Green Chapel](../places/the-green-chapel.md).

More symbol than treasure — it embodies the bargain itself and the suspended threat
Gawain carries north for a year.
