The fellowship of knights gathered at [Camelot](../places/camelot.md) under
[King Arthur](../npcs/king-arthur.md). Bound by a code of **courage and courtesy** —
the two virtues the campaign repeatedly weighs. The Round Table's collective
reputation is the real target of the [Green Knight's](../npcs/the-green-knight.md)
challenge: he comes to see whether its fame is deserved.

Sir Gawain — Arthur's nephew and the player party's exemplar — steps forward on the
Table's behalf, making the whole fellowship's honor ride on his conduct. (In a
Bardcast campaign the players *are* the knights who answer the challenge; Gawain is
the canonical model for that role, not a fixed NPC.)
