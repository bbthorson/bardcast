The appointed place: less a chapel than a grassy barrow or cleft in the earth, by a
loud rushing stream — wild, ominous, and faintly unholy. Here Gawain kneels to
receive the blow he bargained for a year before.

Setting for the climax, [The Green Chapel](../../quest/beats/04-the-green-chapel.md).
The [Green Knight](../npcs/the-green-knight.md) is revealed to be
[Lord Bertilak](../npcs/lord-bertilak.md), and the three axe-feints map directly to
how Gawain handled the three days of temptation at
[Castle Hautdesert](./castle-hautdesert.md). The reckoning measures honesty, not
combat prowess.
