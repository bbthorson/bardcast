# Quest canon

Two arcs the DM steers the party through. The first is *plotted* (the gathering); the
second is *node-based* (the tribulations) — a selectable pool, roughly one episode per
chapter. Each beat names its arc, location, NPCs, the slots it establishes, the central
choice, and the **resolution mode** the dice engine keys off (`docs/story-engine.md §3`).

## Arcs
1. [The Gathering](./arcs/01-the-gathering.md) — plotted prologue
2. [The Tribulations](./arcs/02-the-tribulations.md) — node-based pilgrimage

## Beats
- [The Freed Adept](./beats/00-the-freed-adept.md) — assembly; establishes the disciples
- [The Demon of the Ridge](./beats/t1-demon-of-the-ridge.md) — combat
- [The Thrice-Disguised](./beats/t2-the-thrice-disguised.md) — trickery; the party against itself
- [The Borrowed Beast](./beats/t3-the-borrowed-beast.md) — resolved only by petitioning heaven
