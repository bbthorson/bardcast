## Setting
The threshold of demon-country at
[Five-Elements Mountain](../../world/places/five-elements-mountain.md) — the imperial road
behind, the wild west ahead.

## What happens
[Tripitaka](../../world/npcs/tripitaka.md), sent by [Guanyin](../../world/npcs/guanyin.md),
frees the [Unruly Adept](../../cast/slot-unruly-adept.md) from beneath the mountain.
Grateful but wild, the adept is bound to obedience by the
[golden fillet](../../world/items/the-golden-fillet.md). Over the following stretch the
other disciples — [the Appetite](../../cast/slot-appetite.md) and
[the Steady Penitent](../../cast/slot-steady-penitent.md) — fall in. The party is whole.

## The central choice
*Who is each disciple, and how do they take the leash?* This is the chapter that
**establishes each player-disciple** — the JTTW analogue of Gawain's beat-1. Good DM
prompts: "you are freed after centuries beneath this mountain and a frail monk stands over
you — what do you do?"; "the circlet bites the first time you disobey — what goes through
your mind?" Replies are anchors and backstory; the chapter expands well past them.

## Resolution notes
Largely roleplay and a contest of wills — a Charisma/Wisdom check to set the terms of
service; little to fail lethally. The fillet guarantees the adept can be checked, so the
stakes here are social, not mortal.
