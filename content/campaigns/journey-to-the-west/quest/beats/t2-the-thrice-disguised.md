## Setting
[White-Tiger Ridge](../../world/places/white-tiger-ridge.md), lonely and pale.

## What happens
The [White-Bone Demon](../../world/npcs/the-white-bone-demon.md) approaches the pilgrims
three times — as a girl bearing food, as a grieving crone, as an old man seeking his lost
family — each disguise a ploy to get the [adept](../../cast/slot-unruly-adept.md) sent off
and the [monk](../../world/npcs/tripitaka.md) alone. The adept's true-sight pierces every
disguise and he strikes; the monk sees only slaughtered innocents and, enraged, may invoke
the [fillet](../../world/items/the-golden-fillet.md) and **banish** him.

## The central choice
*Be right and be punished, or be obedient and be wrong?* The demon's weapon is the party's
own mercy and discord — the danger is the party fracturing, not a sword.

## Resolution notes
A social/trick episode: Insight/Perception to read the disguise, Persuasion to make the
monk believe before acting. Failure is a **setback** — the adept banished, the party split
and weakened for a stretch — recoverable, not lethal. A natural **decision node** for the
backtrack design (`docs/story-engine.md §4`): banishing the adept is a real, reversible
choice with consequences.
