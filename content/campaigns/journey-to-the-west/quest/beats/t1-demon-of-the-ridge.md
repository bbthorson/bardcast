## Setting
[Yellow-Wind Ridge](../../world/places/yellow-wind-ridge.md), a wind-scoured stretch of
mountain road.

## What happens
The [Yellow-Wind King](../../world/npcs/the-yellow-wind-king.md) raises a blinding gale,
scatters the party, and seizes [the monk](../../world/npcs/tripitaka.md) for his
cooking-pot. The disciples must regroup and storm the cave to get him back. No disguise, no
celestial twist — strength, tactics, and the
[staff](../../world/items/the-ruyi-staff.md) carry the day.

## The central choice
*How do you fight a foe who can blind and scatter you?* The gale is the wrinkle — a head-on
charge risks the party being split and picked off one by one.

## Resolution notes
A combat episode: Strength/Dexterity attacks vs. the demon's AC, with the gale as a
Constitution/Dexterity save to avoid being scattered (blinded, separated) on a failure — a
**setback**, not death. Losing here means capture and a rescue, not a wipe.

→ back to the road; the DM picks the next [Tribulation](../arcs/02-the-tribulations.md).
