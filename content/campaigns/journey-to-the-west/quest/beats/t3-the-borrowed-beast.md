## Setting
The open road; the [Azure Lion](../../world/npcs/the-azure-lion.md) has seized it.

## What happens
The party fights and **cannot win** — every blow is shrugged off — because the beast is no
true demon but the escaped mount of a lord of
[the Celestial Bureaucracy](../../world/factions/the-celestial-bureaucracy.md). The only
path is for a disciple to leave the fight, fly to heaven, find its master, and have it
[recalled](../../world/npcs/guanyin.md). Victory is **administrative, not martial**.

## The central choice
*When force fails, who swallows their pride and goes for help?* For the
[adept](../../cast/slot-unruly-adept.md) especially, conceding he cannot win and appealing
upward is the hard, characterful choice.

## Resolution notes
A celestial-intervention episode: no AC/HP race wins it. Resolution is investigation (whose
mount is this?) plus a journey/petition — an Arcana/Religion check to identify it, a social
appeal to its master. Failure stalls the road — capture, delay — a **setback** while the
party finds the right door in heaven. Use sparingly, so "call heaven" never becomes the
routine answer.

→ back to the road; the DM picks the next [Tribulation](../arcs/02-the-tribulations.md).
