The pilgrimage proper: largely **self-contained episodes**, selectable rather than
strictly ordered (Alexandrian node-based design, `docs/canon-compiler.md §5`). The DM
picks which tribulation comes next; the journey-west framing is the only ordering
constraint. Three representative episodes are seeded — deliberately spanning **combat**,
**trickery**, and a foe beatable only by **petitioning heaven** — to exercise the
resolution vocabulary.

**Node-based completeness:** because episodes are selectable, the linter checks **path
redundancy** (Three Clue Rule) — each is reachable from the road and none is a chokepoint.
