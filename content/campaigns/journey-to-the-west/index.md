# Journey to the West — campaign bundle

A Bardcast campaign seed, projected as an [OKF](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/knowledge-catalog/blob/main/okf/SPEC.md)
bundle. Source: Wu Cheng'en's 16th-century novel *Xiyouji* (public domain). All prose
here is original summary; no copyrighted translation is reproduced.

This is **world + quest canon** — Bardcast-staff-authored, licensing-reviewed,
read-only to DM clients (see `docs/story-engine.md §1`). It is the **second test case**
for the canon compiler (`docs/canon-compiler.md`): an ensemble, episodic, **node-based**
pilgrimage chosen to contrast with the linear, single-bargain spine of *Sir Gawain and
the Green Knight*.
