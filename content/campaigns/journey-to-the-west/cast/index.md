# Cast — player-fillable slots

The disciple roles a player inhabits. Each is a `CharacterSlot` with Propp `role: hero`
and a set of **drives** — the JTTW-demanded first-class field, since the companions'
wants are explicit where Gawain's are implicit. The named figure in each is the canonical
*model*, not a fixed NPC; players bring their own character into the slot.

- [The Unruly Adept](./slot-unruly-adept.md) — modeled on the stone-born monkey sage
- [The Appetite](./slot-appetite.md) — modeled on the gluttonous pig-spirit
- [The Steady Penitent](./slot-steady-penitent.md) — modeled on the river-ogre turned monk
