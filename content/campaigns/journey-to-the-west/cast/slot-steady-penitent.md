The quiet one: once a fearsome river-ogre, now a disciplined penitent who shoulders the
baggage, holds the line, and mediates between the [adept](./slot-unruly-adept.md) and
[the appetite](./slot-appetite.md). Rarely the spotlight, often the reason the party
survives.

**Canonical model:** Sha Wujing, the sand-monk. A player here should be steady, dutiful,
and quietly capable — the emotional ballast.

**Drives shape the dice:** duty and atonement steer toward the hard-but-right choice;
keeping-the-peace is both a strength and, at the wrong moment, a hesitation.
