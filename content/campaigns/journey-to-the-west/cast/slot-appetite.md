The party's appetite made flesh: strong but lazy, forever scheming to eat, rest, or
abandon the quest and go home. He grumbles, he's gulled by any pretty disguise, and he
sows discord with the [adept](./slot-unruly-adept.md) — yet at the breaking point he
fights for the party.

**Canonical model:** Zhu Bajie, the pig-spirit. A player here should be earthy, funny,
and self-interested, with a loyalty that surprises everyone (including himself).

**Drives shape the dice:** hunger and comfort pull toward the easy, wrong choice;
loyalty-when-it-counts is the redemption the campaign rewards.
