The party's powerhouse: vast strength, transformations, and an eye that sees through
disguise. Chafes at authority and is quick to violence — exactly what the demons exploit
and what the monk's mercy keeps checking. Held in line by the
[golden fillet](../world/items/the-golden-fillet.md), a literal headache when he
disobeys.

**Canonical model:** Sun Wukong, the stone-born Monkey King. A player in this slot should
be powerful, proud, and impatient — the one most likely to *see* the trap and most likely
to be disbelieved.

**Drives shape the dice:** pride and proving-worth push bold action; hard-won loyalty is
the arc the campaign tests (drives → sheet traits → modifiers, `docs/story-engine.md §3`).
