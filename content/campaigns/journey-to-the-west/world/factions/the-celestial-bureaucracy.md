Heaven run as an immense civil service of deities and officials, each with mounts,
servants, and treasures. A recurring engine of the pilgrimage: a "demon" is often a
**celestial's escaped servant** (the [Azure Lion](../npcs/the-azure-lion.md)), so the fix
is to find its master and have it recalled — the *celestial-intervention* resolution.
[Guanyin](../npcs/guanyin.md) is the party's usual point of contact.
