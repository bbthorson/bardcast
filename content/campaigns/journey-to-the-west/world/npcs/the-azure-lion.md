A mighty beast-demon who has seized a stretch of the road. The party cannot defeat him —
every blow is shrugged off — because he is no true demon but the **escaped mount of a lord
of [the Celestial Bureaucracy](../factions/the-celestial-bureaucracy.md)**. The only
solution is for a disciple to fly to heaven and have his master [recalled](./guanyin.md). A
villain whose defeat is **administrative, not martial**.
