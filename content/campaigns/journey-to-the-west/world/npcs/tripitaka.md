A holy man of boundless compassion and very little worldly sense. Charged by
[Guanyin](./guanyin.md) to fetch the scriptures, he leads the pilgrimage in name but
depends utterly on his disciples to survive it. His **mercy is the recurring hazard**: he
believes disguised demons, rebukes the [adept](../../cast/slot-unruly-adept.md) for
"killing innocents," and alone knows the charm that works the
[golden fillet](../items/the-golden-fillet.md).

**Propp role:** dispatcher (he carries the quest) crossed with the *sought/protected*
figure (his safety is the stake). Never a combatant.
