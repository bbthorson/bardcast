The bodhisattva who arranged the pilgrimage, recruited the disciples, and gave the monk
the [golden fillet](../items/the-golden-fillet.md) to keep the
[adept](../../cast/slot-unruly-adept.md) in check. When the party meets a foe beyond force
— often a celestial's escaped servant — she is the one petitioned to set it right.

**Propp role:** donor and dispatcher — she provides the magical means and sends the heroes
out. Use sparingly: she is the engine of the *celestial-intervention* resolution mode, and
overuse cheapens it.
