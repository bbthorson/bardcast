A powerful demon who holds [Yellow-Wind Ridge](../places/yellow-wind-ridge.md), conjures a
blinding gale, and carries the [monk](./tripitaka.md) off to his cave to be eaten. A
**straight antagonist** — no disguise, no celestial complication: he is beaten by strength,
cunning, and the [adept's staff](../items/the-ruyi-staff.md), though his wind can scatter
the party first.
