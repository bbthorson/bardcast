A demon of [White-Tiger Ridge](../places/white-tiger-ridge.md) who cannot win by force, so
she wins by **deception**: she approaches the pilgrims three times in three innocent
disguises. The [adept](../../cast/slot-unruly-adept.md) sees through each and strikes — and
the merciful [monk](./tripitaka.md), seeing only slain innocents, rebukes and even
**banishes** him. The real adversary in this episode is the party's own discord.
