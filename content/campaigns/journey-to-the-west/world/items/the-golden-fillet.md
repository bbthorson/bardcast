A band given by [Guanyin](../npcs/guanyin.md) and tricked onto the
[adept's](../../cast/slot-unruly-adept.md) head. At a charm only
[Tripitaka](../npcs/tripitaka.md) knows, it constricts and brings the strongest disciple to
his knees. It encodes the campaign's central **party tension** as an object: power leashed
by mercy. Expect it invoked exactly when the adept is most sure he is right.
