A miraculous iron staff that grows, shrinks, and multiplies at its wielder's word — the
[Unruly Adept's](../../cast/slot-unruly-adept.md) weapon and emblem. In combat episodes it
is the party's decisive force; against disguise and against celestial foes it is useless,
which is precisely the point of those episodes.
