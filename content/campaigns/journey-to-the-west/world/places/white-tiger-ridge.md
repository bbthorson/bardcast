A desolate ridge of pale rock, hunting-ground of the
[White-Bone Demon](../npcs/the-white-bone-demon.md), who preys not by force but by
**disguise** — appearing as a harmless traveler to get close to the tender-hearted
[monk](../npcs/tripitaka.md). The danger here is the party's own mercy and discord, not a
demon's claws.
