A bleak ridge where a yellow gale blinds and scatters travelers. The
[Yellow-Wind King](../npcs/the-yellow-wind-king.md) rules it from a cave-hall and seizes
the [monk](../npcs/tripitaka.md) to eat him. Little subtlety here — the road is blocked by
force and cleared by force.
