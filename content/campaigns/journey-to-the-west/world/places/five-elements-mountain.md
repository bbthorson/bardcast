A great mountain that has held the [Unruly Adept](../../cast/slot-unruly-adept.md)
trapped beneath it for ages, penance for an old rebellion against heaven. When
[Tripitaka](../npcs/tripitaka.md) passes on his way west, he lifts the seal and frees the
adept — who becomes his first and mightiest disciple, bound to obedience by the
[golden fillet](../items/the-golden-fillet.md).

The campaign's threshold: the imperial road and safety behind, demon-country ahead.
